The One Ratio That Decides It

A DSCR lender never asks for your W-2s, pay stubs, or tax returns. The property qualifies, not you, and it comes down to one line of arithmetic:

monthly rent ÷ full monthly payment = DSCR

The payment side counts everything: principal, interest, taxes, insurance, and any HOA. At 1.00, the property carries itself. Most programs fund from around 1.00 up, and pricing gets noticeably better at 1.25 and above.
